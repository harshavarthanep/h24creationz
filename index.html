      Questions clients ask

      Can you build a brand completely from scratch?

      Yes. Name, logo, tagline, colours and type, website, brochure, business cards with QR, social media handles and templates, and a Google Maps listing, all designed as one consistent kit.

      We already have a brand. Can you refresh it without losing what people know?

      Yes. A rebrand can be a careful refresh or a full change. We keep what customers already recognise and fix what's holding you back.

      What is GEO, and does my business need it?

      GEO, or generative engine optimization, makes your business easier for AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews to find, understand and recommend. It builds on good SEO, so we set up both together.

      Can you get my business on Google Maps?

      Yes. We set up or fix your Google Business Profile with correct details, categories, photos and services, so nearby customers can find you, call you and get directions.

      How much does a project cost?

      Every project is quoted in writing after a short conversation, based on what you need. You approve scope, dates and cost before any work starts.

      How long does a project take?

      It depends on the scope. You get clear dates in the written plan before any work starts, and updates as each stage is done.

      Can you set up WhatsApp automation or an AI chat assistant?

      Yes. We can route website enquiries into WhatsApp, send instant replies and add an assistant that answers common questions, so no lead waits for a reply.

      Do you work with businesses outside Chennai?

      Yes. Work happens over calls, WhatsApp and email. OCB, for example, is a clothing brand in the USA.

      Who owns the website and files?

      You do. The domain, files and logins are in your name and handed over whenever you want them.
